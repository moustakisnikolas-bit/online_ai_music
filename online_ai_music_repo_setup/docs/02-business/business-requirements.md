# Business Requirements

## Business Goal
Create a sustainable media business based on original ambient audio and related digital assets.

## Revenue Sources
- Streaming royalties
- Video monetization
- Digital downloads
- Licensing
- Future SaaS subscriptions

### YouTube monetization gate (verified 2026-07, not assumed)

Uploading to YouTube does not earn anything by itself -- a channel has to
qualify for the YouTube Partner Program (YPP) first, and eligibility is a
two-tier system:

- **Tier 1 (early access, limited features)**: 500 subscribers **and**
  either 3,000 valid public watch hours in the last 12 months **or**
  3,000,000 Shorts views in the last 90 days.
- **Tier 2 (full monetization)**: 1,000 subscribers **and** either 4,000
  valid public watch hours in the last 12 months **or** 10,000,000 Shorts
  views in the last 90 days.

Also required regardless of tier: two-step verification enabled on the
account, no active Community Guidelines strikes, compliance with YouTube's
monetization policies, and residing in a country/region where YPP is
available. Applications typically take about a month to review.

Practical implication: uploading consistently and building an audience
comes before any YouTube revenue exists at all -- factor that lead time
into any income projection, and don't expect the ~$238/mo LabelGrid
revisit trigger (see the roadmap's distributor section) to be reachable
from YouTube alone in the early period.

## Constraints
- Respect platform policies.
- No fake engagement or stream manipulation.
- Maintain provenance for generated assets.
